Training:

  • Dropout: 0.4

  • Hardware: Apple Silicon MPS
  • Training time: ~4 hours

Deployment:
  • Mode: DETERMINISTIC (production)
  • Temperature: 0.6617 (calibrated)
  • Inference speed: 0.1 seconds per case
  • Model size: ~300 MB
  • Dependencies: PyTorch, torchvision, PIL
